Join or Guest

Fe Nix is accepting new members all the time! By joining our clan in game, you can chat with the community in game, rank up as your account progresses, share in the success of others, and compete in our weekly competitions! If you want to try it out without becoming a member, or you're already in another clan, you can also join as a guest. However, guests miss out on competitions, ranks, and seeing level up and other achievement announcements.

Make sure to read the rules below, and then you can join or guest in game.

Rules

  • Treat each other with respect, if someone asks you to stop, please stop.
  • Fe Nix is; anti-racist, anti-misogynist, pro-LGBT. Words/actions in conflict with those values are ban-able.
  • Discuss sensitive subjects with care. topics to be mindful of include; addictive substances, guns, violence, politics, and religion.
  • Follow all RuneScape rules.
  • Do not crash fellow clan members while grinding.
  • PVP between clan members should be agreed to by both sides.
  • Each person can have at most two ranked accounts in the clan at a time, one of which has the alt rank.
